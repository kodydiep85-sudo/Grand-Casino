HOW TO PLAY

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their number, J/Q/K count 10, and an Ace counts 1 or 11. Up to five players each play their own hand against the dealer.

1 · Bet

Tap a chip, then BET in your seat ($5–$500). The Pair Bonus is optional (up to $100). Then tap Deal; at a friends' table, Deal me in. Pick a 6-deck shoe or double deck: a fresh shoe is shuffled when the cut card comes out.

Cards go out one at a time, starting with the seat on the dealer's left (on the right of your screen): one card each, the dealer's up card, a second card each, then the dealer's hole card face down.

2 · The Bust Bonus

Once the dealer's card is out, the Bust Bonus spot lights up: bet up to $100 that the dealer busts. It pays the same however many cards the dealer takes to bust, with an extra bonus when every card in the dealer's busted hand is the same suit. When the dealer shows an Ace you can also take insurance (half your bet, pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack). With a ten or an Ace showing, the dealer then checks for blackjack.

3 · Play your hand

  • Hit for another card, or Stand.
  • Double: double your bet on your first two cards and take exactly one more card.
  • Split two cards of the same value into two hands (another bet the same size), up to four hands. Split Aces get one card each. You can double after a split.

At a friends' table you have 15 seconds for the Bust Bonus and insurance, and 30 seconds a turn (if time runs out you stand).

4 · The dealer

The dealer turns over the hole card and draws until 17 or more, standing on all 17s. Blackjack (an Ace and a ten-card) pays 3 to 2. Beat the dealer and you're paid 1 to 1; a tie pushes.
