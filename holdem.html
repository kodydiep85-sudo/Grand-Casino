HOW TO PLAY

Up to five players each play their own hand against the dealer. Everyone uses their two cards plus the five shared board cards to make the best five-card poker hand.

1 · Bet

Tap a chip, then Ante in your seat. An equal Blind bet goes on with it. Trips (your final hand), Pair Bonus (your two cards) and the $5 jackpot light are optional. Then tap Deal. At a friends' table, tap Deal me in: the cards go out when everyone is ready, or when the one-minute betting clock runs out.

The five board cards go out face down first, then the players get one card at a time, starting with the seat on the dealer's left (first base, on the right of your screen) and going around the table twice, then the dealer's two.

2 · Decide when to raise

Tap your cards to turn them over. One card showing is enough to bet; the other turns over at the end.

  1. Before the flop: bet 3× or 4× your Ante on Play, or check.
  2. After the flop (three board cards): if you checked, bet 2× or check again.
  3. After the river (all five board cards): if you still checked, bet 1× or fold.

You make one Play bet a hand. The board turns over once every player has decided. At a friends' table you have 30 seconds for each decision; if time runs out you check (or fold on the river).

3 · Showdown

  • The dealer needs a pair or better to qualify. If not, your Ante pushes, and the other bets are settled normally.
  • Beat the dealer: Ante (if the dealer qualifies) and Play pay 1 to 1, and Blind pays by the table below.
  • Lose to the dealer: you lose Ante, Blind and Play. Tie: they all push.

Every $5 bet goes onto the jackpot the moment the light goes on, so the meter climbs as people play. It starts at $100,000 and never resets: it keeps building, even after someone wins. The whole jackpot pays only for a royal flush made with your two cards and the flop. A royal flush made later pays 10% of the jackpot. The other hands count your final hand.
