Privacy Policy

Grand Squeeze Baccarat — last updated October 2026. Replace the contact details below with your own before publishing.

Summary

The game does not ask for an account, email address, location, contacts or payment details, and it contains no advertising or analytics.

What is stored on your device

Your chosen display name and your solo chip balance are saved on your own device so they are there next time. You can clear them by deleting the app or its data.

Online tables

When you start or join a table with friends, the game connects to our table server. The server receives the display name you typed, your bets and your moves for that table only. This information is held in memory while the table is open and is discarded when everyone leaves. It is not sold or shared, and it is not used to identify you.

Virtual chips

Chips are virtual, have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, transferred or redeemed. The game offers no real-money gambling.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
